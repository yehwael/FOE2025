CPE211 Core Mathematical Formulas Reference

1. Energy Units & Mechanical Power

$1\text{ kWh} = 3.6 \times 10^6\text{ J} = 3412\text{ Btu}$

$1\text{ Btu} = 1055\text{ J} \quad \mid \quad 1\text{ cal} = 4.184\text{ J}$

$1\text{ toe} = 4.5 \times 10^{10}\text{ J} \quad \mid \quad 1\text{ barrel} = 6 \times 10^9\text{ J}$

$PE = mgh \quad \mid \quad KE = \frac{1}{2} m V^2$

2. Solar Photovoltaic (PV) Systems

$\eta_{\text{cell}} = \frac{P_{\text{out}}}{P_{\text{in}}} = \frac{V \cdot I}{G \cdot A}$

$\eta(T) = \eta_{\text{ref}} \left[ 1 - \beta_{\text{ref}} (T - T_{\text{ref}}) \right]$

$\text{Series: } V_T = \sum V_i, \quad I_T = \min(I_1, I_2, \dots)$

$\text{Parallel: } I_T = \sum I_i, \quad V_T = \min(V_1, V_2, \dots)$

3. Wind Turbine & Ocean Wave Power

$P_{\text{wind, inlet}} = \frac{1}{2} \rho A V^3 = \frac{1}{2} \rho \left(\frac{\pi}{4} D^2\right) V^3$

$P_{\text{elec}} = P_{\text{wind, inlet}} \cdot C_p \cdot \eta_m \cdot \eta_g \quad (\text{Betz: } C_{p,\max} = \frac{16}{27} \approx 0.593)$

$P_{\text{wave}} = \frac{\rho A g^2 a^2}{8\pi} \sqrt{\frac{2\pi\lambda}{g}}$

$\text{Wave Amplitude } a = \frac{H}{2} \quad (\text{Half of vertical height})$

4. Internal Combustion Engines (ICE)

$IP = BP + FP \implies FP = IP - BP \quad (\text{Constant at partial loads})$

$\eta_m = \frac{BP}{IP} \quad \mid \quad \eta_{m, x\%} = \frac{x \cdot BP_{\text{full}}}{(x \cdot BP_{\text{full}}) + FP}$

$\eta_{\text{ith}} = \frac{IP}{\dot{m}_f \cdot C.V.} \quad \mid \quad \eta_{\text{bth}} = \frac{BP}{\dot{m}_f \cdot C.V.}$

$\text{Air-Fuel Ratio (AFR)} = \frac{\dot{m}_a}{\dot{m}_f}$

5. HVAC, Carnot Limits & Rankine Steam Power Plants

$Q_H = Q_L + W \quad \mid \quad COP_{HP} = COP_R + 1$

$COP_{R, \text{Carnot}} = \frac{T_L}{T_H - T_L} \quad (\text{Temperatures strictly in Kelvin})$

$\eta_{\text{Rankine}} = \frac{W_{\text{net}}}{Q_{\text{boiler}}} = \frac{W_{\text{turbine}} - W_{\text{pump}}}{Q_{\text{boiler}}}$

$\eta_{\text{overall}} = \frac{P_{\text{elec, net}}}{\dot{m}_f \cdot C.V.}$
